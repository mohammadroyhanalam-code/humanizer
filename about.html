About Humanizer

Humanizer is a free, offline tool that rewrites AI-sounding text to sound more natural and human. Paste AI text, pick a style (Balanced, Casual, Formal, Concise, Creative), set intensity, and hit Humanize.

How it works: the default version runs 100% in your browser. It strips common AI cliches (like delve, tapestry, leverage, moreover), adds contractions, swaps synonyms, and splits long sentences for natural burstiness. It also shows an AI-likelihood heuristic score, word counts, and a rewrite log.

There is also an optional LLM-powered version for higher quality if you bring your own free API key. Your key stays in your browser only.

No signup. No uploads for the offline version. Free to use.
